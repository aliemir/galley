Know when an agent is waiting for your feedback.

- When an agent is waiting for your feedback — `galley feedback --wait` or `--watch`, or an agent you asked to *keep watching for my feedback* — **Save Feedback** shows a green dot, and its tooltip says who: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and others. Saving reaches it at once.
- The dot appears only when the agent watches a folder that holds your drafts, so it never promises a save that would go nowhere.

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. From Galley 1.1, choose Galley → Check for Updates….
