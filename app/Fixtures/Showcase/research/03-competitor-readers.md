# Competitor readers

A quick look at what people use today to read agent output.

## Observations

Most readers are editors in disguise. They open a file, not a folder, and none
of them remember what you already read. The few that watch a folder show a
line diff — useful for code, tiring for prose.

## What we keep

- Read-only by design
- A folder is the unit, not a file
- Changes shown in the text, not beside it
